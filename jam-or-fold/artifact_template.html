Method & fidelity to the book how this was computed

Equities are exact: every one of the 812,175 distinct hand-vs-hand matchups was reduced by suit isomorphism to 50,258 classes and enumerated over all C(48,5) = 1,712,304 boards (the 7-card evaluator was verified against the known frequency counts of all 133,784,560 seven-card hands). Equilibria are exact linear programs over behavioral strategies with full card-removal accounting, solved independently for both players at every stack size; the two LP values agree to <10-6 at every point.
