What position is worth — and how deep it stops mattering

The one-street no-limit AKQ game, solved exactly at 300 stack sizes. The player in position profits at every depth — but the edge climbs only until the stack is about half a pot, then freezes at +0.0272 pots and never moves again. Drag the stack to watch the equilibrium reorganize.

The value of position vs. stack depth Y’s equilibrium profit, in pots — the whole story lives under one pot

Four regimes, shaded. Two have exact closed-form laws (dashed): jam-only S²/(6(1+S)) and the polar-bet law (S/(1+S)−(3−2√2))/6, which meet tangent at S = √2−1. The curve rises, corners, and goes perfectly flat at S* = 0.525 — exactly Y’s own largest bet size.

How often each line gets played Betting-line frequencies across stack depth

Deep-stack play is dominated by check–check (39%) and the check–bet lines: X often checks its merged range and lets Y drive. The raise lines (b–r) — Y punishing X’s blocking bet — appear only once the stack is deep enough to raise into.

The four regimesReading upward from an all-in short stack

I · ends at √2−1

Jam-or-fold

0 < S ≤ 0.414

Bets are tiny next to the pot, so everything collapses to jam-or-call. X jams every Ace and some Queens; the King never jams.

V = S² / (6(1+S))
II · polar

The classical bet

0.414 ≤ S ≤ 0.483

The stack covers the optimal bet. X bets √2−1 of pot with a polar range (nut Aces + Queen bluffs); Y jams behind. Hands off from regime I with matching value and slope.

V = (S/(1+S) − (3−2√2)) / 6
III · Player X strikes back

The King’s blocker

0.483 ≤ S ≤ 0.525

The book’s move switches on: X’s King makes a small blocking bet. The bet size collapses from 0.41 toward 0.16 as the range merges. Y’s raise keeps it honest.

no closed form — fast mixture
IV · plateau

Depth stops mattering

S ≥ 0.525

Value freezes at +0.0272 pots and every strategy locks. Onset = Y’s largest bet size: once chips cover that, more stack is dead weight. Flat to 10⁻¹⁶.

V = constant

MethodHow the numbers are made

Each stack size is solved exactly by sequence-form linear programming — both players’ LPs, with a strong-duality check. No-limit bet and raise sizes are discretized on a grid refined around the equilibrium support until the value stops moving; raises are capped at one and validated against deeper caps (correction < 3×10⁻⁵). The three short-stack regime laws are hand-derived and verified against the LP to ~10⁻¹⁷. The King’s blocking bet and the raises that punish it are Chen & Ankenman’s results from ch. 15 (“Player X Strikes Back”); the exact bet sizes, ranges, the four-regime structure, and the plateau are this project’s extension.
